## 3. Personal data we collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

### 3.1 Customer data (mandatory for service delivery)
- **Identity:** full name, gender, date of birth (where age verification is required)
- **Contact:** phone number(s), email address, residential / delivery address, GPS coordinates of delivery locations
- **Transactional:** purchase history, payment method, bank account details (for refunds), cylinder ownership records, delivery preferences

### 3.2 Customer data (optional, with explicit consent)
- **Marketing preferences:** opt-in / opt-out per channel (SMS, WhatsApp, email)
- **Profiling data:** purchase frequency, lifetime gas volume, segment classification (Retail, Commercial, Distributor, Bulk), inferred customer lifetime value
- **Feedback:** customer satisfaction ratings, complaint history, free-text comments

### 3.3 Employee, contractor, and vendor data
- **Identity:** full name, photograph, date of birth, gender, marital status, dependants
- **Government IDs:** NIN, BVN (for payroll bank reconciliation), TIN, drivers' license, work permit
- **Statutory:** pension RSA number, NSITF number, HMO enrolment
- **Employment:** designation, grade, salary, bank account, attendance, performance
- **Health:** as needed for occupational health, safety incidents, HMO administration

### 3.4 Technical data (when you use our digital channels)
- Device type, browser, IP address, session timestamps
- Call recordings (when calling our customer care line — see §4.3)
- WhatsApp / SMS message content (limited to the substance of the conversation with us)
- Cookies and similar technologies on our website (separate cookie policy applies)

### 3.5 Sensitive personal data (NDPA s.30, GDPR Art 9)
We **do not** routinely collect sensitive personal data (religion, ethnicity, political opinion, genetic data, sexual orientation, trade union membership). Where exceptionally required (e.g., HMO medical records, religious-leave accommodation), we rely on **explicit consent** and store such data with enhanced security controls.

## 4. Lawful basis for processing (NDPA s.25)

We process personal data only where one or more of the following lawful bases apply:

| Purpose | Lawful basis |
|---|---|
| Selling LPG and fulfilling orders | **Contract** (NDPA s.25(b)) |
| Payment processing and accounting records | **Legal obligation** (Tax Acts, NIBSS, anti-money-laundering laws) |
| Routing your interaction to the right CSE / agent | **Legitimate interest** in efficient service delivery |
| Sending you transactional messages (delivery confirmation, receipts) | **Contract** |
| Sending you marketing SMS / WhatsApp / email | **Consent** (you may withdraw at any time — see §8) |
| Customer profiling for segmentation and engagement | **Legitimate interest**, balanced against your rights (see §4.1) |
| Churn detection and re-engagement campaigns | **Legitimate interest** + **Consent** for outreach |
| Recording calls for quality and training | **Legitimate interest** + notice to caller |
| Employee payroll, tax, pension, HMO | **Contract**, **Legal obligation** |
| Background checks during recruitment | **Consent**, **Legitimate interest** |
| Responding to your queries and complaints | **Contract**, **Legitimate interest** |
| CCTV at our outlets | **Legitimate interest** in safety and asset protection |

### 4.1 Automated decision-making and profiling (NDPA s.37, GDPR Art 22)

We use automated logic to:
- Classify customers by segment (Retail / Commercial / Distributor / Bulk) for routing purposes;
- Detect customers at risk of churn based on their purchase cycle;
- Calculate Pay-As-You-Earn (PAYE) tax deductions on employee salaries.

These automated processes do **not** make decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects on you without human review. You have the right to request human intervention, express your point of view, and contest any decision (see §8).

## 6. How long we keep your data (retention)

We keep personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, plus any additional period required by law:

| Data category | Retention period |
|---|---|
| Active customer transactional records | **7 years** after last transaction (Companies and Allied Matters Act, Tax Acts) |
| Inactive customer records (no transaction for 7 years) | Anonymised or deleted |
| Marketing consent records | **7 years** after withdrawal (to evidence consent history) |
| Customer service interactions, tickets, feedback | **3 years** after closure |
| Call recordings | **90 days**, unless retained longer for a specific ticket or legal matter |
| Employee records (active) | Duration of employment + **10 years** (post-termination, for pension, tax, litigation) |
| Job applicant records (unsuccessful) | **6 months** after recruitment decision, unless you consent to longer for talent pool |
| CCTV footage | **30 days** (overwritten thereafter) unless retained for a specific incident |
| Website analytics | **26 months** (Google Analytics default; configurable) |

At end of retention, data is securely deleted or irreversibly anonymised.

## 8. Your rights (NDPA s.34-39, GDPR Art 15-22)

You have the right to:

- **Access** — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
- **Rectification** — ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data
- **Erasure** ("right to be forgotten") — ask us to delete your data where there is no overriding lawful basis to retain
- **Restriction** — ask us to pause processing while we investigate a dispute
- **Portability** — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format and transmit it to another controller
- **Object** — to processing based on legitimate interest, or to direct marketing (we will stop immediately)
- **Withdraw consent** — at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing
- **Not be subject to solely automated decisions** that produce legal or significant effects on you
- **Lodge a complaint** with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (see §11)

**How to exercise your rights:**

- Email: **dpo@sungas.org** (see §10 for full DPO contact)
- WhatsApp / SMS: send "STOP" to opt out of marketing on that channel; the request will be logged in our Do Not Contact register within 24 hours
- In writing: Office of the DPO, Sungas Company Limited, **[INSERT ADDRESS]**

We will respond within **30 days** (NDPA s.34(2)) and free of charge, unless your request is manifestly unfounded or excessive.

## 9. How we protect your data

We apply the following security measures:

- Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256) for all personal data
- Role-based access control (RBAC) — staff access only data they need for their job
- Multi-factor authentication for administrative accounts
- Regular security audits and vulnerability scans
- Encrypted backups stored separately from production
- Staff training on data protection (annual)
- Incident response plan with **72-hour breach notification** to NDPC (per NDPA s.40)
- Vendor due diligence and signed Data Processing Agreements

Despite these measures, no system is 100% secure. In the unlikely event of a data breach affecting your data, we will notify you and the NDPC without undue delay where required by law.

## Annex A — Glossary

- **Data subject** — the identifiable individual whose personal data is processed (i.e., you)
- **Personal data** — any information relating to an identifiable individual
- **Processing** — any operation performed on personal data (collection, storage, use, disclosure, deletion)
- **Controller** — the entity that determines the purposes and means of processing (Sungas)
- **Processor** — a third party that processes personal data on behalf of the Controller (e.g., Twilio)
- **Consent** — freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous indication of agreement
- **DNC** — Do Not Contact register; a list of phone numbers and email addresses that have opted out of marketing
